About CTF Copilot

CTF Copilot began as a one-night build sprint by Jack Nelson, designed to turn chaotic challenge workflows into one clean, repeatable solving interface. The idea was simple: keep all challenge context, tools, uploads, and solve state in one place so execution stays focused from first recon to final flag.

The first working version was built in a hotel room marathon, with development running deep into the session until 3:30 PM. Jack orchestrated Claude Code and Codex in parallel while he stepped back to meditate and direct the architecture, turning rapid iterations into a polished CTF workflow engine in a single push.

That same momentum carried into competition, where Jack Nelson took first place at the Microsoft Observer CTF during VMI Cyber Fusion 2026, the morning after he built it. Jack also credits his work with Enzo Hins during the CTF, whose awesome collaboration and support helped inspire key parts of the build. CTF Copilot is the product of that mindset: precise tooling, disciplined execution, and relentless focus on winning.

Note: I recommend meditating, and this app helps. Students can get a free scholarship here: https://app.wakingup.com/scholarship
